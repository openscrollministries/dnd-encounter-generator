# Quick Encounter Generator (D&D 5e)

A free, single-page random encounter generator for Dungeon Masters. Pick party size, level, setting, monster type, and difficulty (or type a request like "4 monsters for 5 third level characters in a swamp") and get balanced monsters with full stat blocks, a difficulty rating (DMG XP thresholds), a suspense hook, a trap, NPCs, and treasure. Includes a standalone treasure roller: Individual Treasure or Treasure Hoard by CR band (coins, gems, art objects, and SRD 5.1 magic items from tables A–I by rarity), an XP tracker (Defeated buttons, manual adds, XP split per party member, per-character totals with level-up alerts; saved in your browser), and a monster lookup with autocomplete over all 334 SRD monsters, plus 10 original homebrew monsters (labeled "Homebrew (original)", filterable). A "Build Your Own Encounter" section at the top lets you pick exact monsters and quantities (e.g. 3 Goblins + 1 Bugbear). Works offline: just open `index.html`.

**Live:** https://openscrollministries.github.io/dnd-encounter-generator/
